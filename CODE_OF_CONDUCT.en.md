# Code of Conduct

[Русский](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · **English**

We are committed to a professional, welcoming, and safe community regardless of experience, background, identity, or viewpoint.

Contributors are expected to:

- discuss ideas and code respectfully;
- give specific technical feedback;
- take responsibility for mistakes and correct them;
- protect other people's data, secrets, and intellectual property;
- never publish customer infobases, configurations, passwords, or unsanitized logs.

Insults, harassment, threats, discrimination, and disclosure of private information are unacceptable. Report security concerns privately according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner through a private channel listed on their GitHub profile. The owner may remove content, restrict participation, or ban a contributor. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This document is a concise project adaptation of the principles in [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
